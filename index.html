I am an undergraduate researcher in Computer Science at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University and a DoD SMART Scholar. My work focuses on machine learning, reinforcement learning, optimization, cybersecurity, and remote sensing, with applications in satellite trajectory optimization, intrusion detection, and autonomous maritime robotics.

I currently intern at the Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD), where I develop real-time autonomous tracking systems that integrate machine learning, computer vision, and radar sensing.

Broadly, I am interested in:

  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence
  • AI for Mathematics (AI4Math), specifically symbolic reasoning and theorem proving for mathematical research
  • Optimization and new learning algorithms (e.g., magnitude-invariant optimizers)
  • Reinforcement learning for safety-critical and physical systems
  • Cybersecurity, intrusion detection, and threat hunting with RL
  • Signal-centric remote sensing for sonar and radar
  • Autonomous aerial and underwater vehicles
