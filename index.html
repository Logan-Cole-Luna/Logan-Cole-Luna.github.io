I am an M.S. Computer Science student (Artificial Intelligence specialization, thesis track) at the Georgia Institute of Technology, a B.S. Computer Science graduate (magna cum laude, Honors Program) of Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, and a DoW SMART Scholar.

My research is in AI for Mathematical Reasoning (AI4Math). I study how semantically informative training signals can be quantified and incorporated into the training and generation of mathematical reasoning models. Symbolic feedback rewards output that compiles or type-checks, but that does not certify that it expresses the intended mathematics. In Georgia Tech's Reasoning and Learning Group under Dr. Vijay Ganesh, I contribute to AIProver, an agentic system that post-trains an open-weight LLM on graded verifier rewards to auto-formalize research mathematics in Lean.

I am also a Scientist Intern at the Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD) (Active Secret clearance), where I build real-time video detection and tracking systems for small, low-visibility targets.

Current research directions

  1. Reinforcement learning from symbolic and semantic-equivalence feedback for auto-formalization
  2. Dense rewards from distance in a learned proof-semantic space, grounded in kernel-checked entailment
  3. Diffusion language models for theorem proving and auto-formalization

Earlier work covers reinforcement learning for safety-critical systems (satellite collision avoidance, intrusion detection, sim-to-real drone control), sonar and remote sensing, and computer vision for autonomous vehicles.
