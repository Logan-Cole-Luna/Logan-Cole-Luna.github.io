  1. AIProver: Agentic Auto-Formalization of Mathematical Research via Certificate-Driven Evolving Harness

    Under reviewICLR 2027

    Prithwish Jana, Viet Bach Hoang, Logan Luna, Viresh Pati, Akash Singirikonda, Cy Xie, Lisa Carbone, Wuyang Chen, Walter Moreira, Joe Stubbs, Sriram Vishwanath, Vijay Ganesh.

    Contribution: RL pipeline and reward, BEq+ extension, distillation pipeline and dataset, Semantic Alignment Model. Results: pass@4 semantic correctness from 15.7% to 36.7% standalone (above GPT-5.6-Sol and Codex); 41.9% to 79.8% as a Claude Code skill.

  2. Dueling Deep Q-Learning for Intrusion Detection

    ConferenceBest Paper Award

    Logan Luna, Matthew P. Berkowitz, Laxima Niure Kandel, Sirio Jansen-Sánchez.

    IEEE SoutheastCon 2025

    99.68% multi-class accuracy on 2.18M CIC-IDS2018 flows, against 88% for prior DQN-based detection and 98.4–99% for tree-ensemble and random-forest IDSs; SHAP attribution of the learned policy.

  3. Satellite Trajectory Optimization via Proximal Policy Optimization for Space Debris Avoidance

    JournalPublished, IEEE Access

    Logan Luna, Juan Ortiz Couder, Raul Alejandro Vargas-Acosta.

    IEEE Access, 2026

    97.5% collision-avoidance success over 1,000 deterministic GEO episodes, 76.8 and 70.0 percentage points above the rule-based and impulsive Δv baselines.

    DOICode

  4. Direct Policy Transfer from Simulator to Firmware: Verification Boundaries for Learned UAV Autonomy

    Under reviewEA Track 8

    Illia Biblyi, Elsy Fuentes Montiel, Andressa C. Ojeda, Logan Luna, Kolton Galie, Vincent Reggi, Nicolas Andricos, Sirio Jansen-Sánchez, M. Ilhan Akbas.

    Under review, EA Track 8: Operational Boundary Assurance for Trustworthy Agentic and Autonomous Systems

    A verification framework for compiling Isaac Lab policies directly into Crazyflie nano-UAV firmware, with executable checks from observation replay to source-to-binary traceability; 2–3 cm hover altitude error onboard.

  5. Signal-Centric Remote Sensing via Alternative Preprocessing and Acoustic Processing for ML-Driven Applications

    IEEE SoutheastCon 2025, Concord, NC

    91.2% reduction in total sonar processing time (13.1% in filtering alone) against image-based CV processing, while preserving target structure the image pipeline removed.
