I am a B.S. Computer Science graduate of Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University and an M.S. Computer Science student (Artificial Intelligence specialization, thesis track) at the Georgia Institute of Technology, and a DoW SMART Scholar. My work spans deep learning theory and optimization, learning dynamics, reinforcement learning for safety-critical systems, cybersecurity, and remote sensing, with applications in satellite trajectory optimization, intrusion detection, and autonomous maritime robotics.

I currently intern at the Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Division (NSWCDD) (Active Secret clearance), where I develop real-time autonomous tracking systems that integrate machine learning, computer vision, and radar sensing. At Georgia Tech's Reasoning and Learning Group, I also research AI4Math and auto-formalization under Dr. Vijay Ganesh.

Broadly, I am interested in:

  • Machine learning and artificial intelligence
  • AI for Mathematics (AI4Math), specifically symbolic reasoning and theorem proving for mathematical research
  • Optimization and new learning algorithms (e.g., magnitude-invariant optimizers)
  • Reinforcement learning for safety-critical and physical systems
  • Cybersecurity, intrusion detection, and threat hunting with RL
  • Signal-centric remote sensing for sonar and radar
  • Autonomous aerial and underwater vehicles
