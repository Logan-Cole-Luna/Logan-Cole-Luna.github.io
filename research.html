AI for Mathematical Reasoning

My central question is how semantically informative training signals can be quantified and incorporated into the training and generation of mathematical reasoning models. Symbolic feedback rewards output that compiles or type-checks, which does not certify that it expresses the intended mathematics. Current directions:

  1. Extending reinforcement learning from symbolic feedback with semantic alignment as the training objective, measured by pass@k at large k.
  2. Dense rewards from distance in a learned proof-semantic space, grounded in kernel-checked entailment.
  3. Diffusion language models as a generative substrate for theorem proving and auto-formalization.

AIProver: Agentic Auto-Formalization of Mathematical Research via Certificate-Driven Evolving Harness

Under reviewICLR 2027

Prithwish Jana, Viet Bach Hoang, Logan Luna, Viresh Pati, Akash Singirikonda, Cy Xie, Lisa Carbone, Wuyang Chen, Walter Moreira, Joe Stubbs, Sriram Vishwanath, Vijay Ganesh.

Contribution: RL pipeline and reward, BEq+ extension, distillation pipeline and dataset, Semantic Alignment Model. Results: pass@4 semantic correctness from 15.7% to 36.7% standalone (above GPT-5.6-Sol and Codex); 41.9% to 79.8% as a Claude Code skill.

PosterSenior Design, Embry-Riddle, 2026

Logan Luna, Sirio Jansen-Sánchez, Vincent Reggi, Kolton Galie, Nicolas Andricos.

PPO policies trained in NVIDIA Isaac Lab and flown autonomously on Crazyflie 2.1 hardware; hover transferred at 1.3/2.1/0.7 cm real-vs-sim RMSE, while a figure-eight task isolated aggressive horizontal maneuvering as the dominant transfer gap.

Logan Luna, Matthew P. Berkowitz, Laxima Niure Kandel, Sirio Jansen-Sánchez.

Research Posters

Below is a selected collection of research posters from 2022–2026, representing my work across reinforcement learning, optimization, sonar processing, remote sensing, sim-to-real robotics, and aviation NLP.

AERO-Sim2Real: Reinforcement Learning For Sim-to-Real Drone Deployment (2026)

Trains PPO-based flight-control policies in NVIDIA IsaacLab and deploys them on Crazyflie 2.1 nano-quadrotors, quantifying sim-to-real divergence across hover, waypoint-following, and figure-eight trajectory tasks.

Learning Causal Structures from Aviation Accident Narratives (2025)

Compares rule-based NLP, transformer (DistilBERT), and few-shot LLM (Mistral-7B) approaches for extracting causal relationships from NTSB aviation accident narratives.

Satellite Proposal Poster (2025)

Proposes a development framework for satellite anomaly detection, with its motivation, mission impact, and design plan.

Satellite Trajectory Research Poster (2024)

Full research results for PPO-based orbital collision avoidance, including stabilized low-fuel orbits and debris-avoidance maneuvers.

Hierarchical Dueling Q-Learning for Intrusion Detection (2024)

A two-agent Q-learning and Dueling DQN system for classifying and responding to cyberattacks, with near-perfect binary and multi-class accuracy on large intrusion datasets.

Sonar Contrastive Learning Research (2024)

A semi-supervised contrastive learning model for sonar-based object detection, with noise reduction strategies and adaptation potential for radar systems.

Autonomous Maritime Robotics Association Poster (2023)

Early work on underwater sonar-based detection, noise filtering strategies, and object tracking; the foundation of later remote-sensing and sonar research.

Aircraft Detection & Kalman Tracking Poster (2023)

Combines YOLO object detection with ORB keypoints and Kalman filtering for aircraft motion prediction. Presented at NCUR 2023; 1st Place at the Embry-Riddle Student Research Symposium.
