Brief Bio

I am a B.S. Computer Science student (Honors Program) with a minor in Computational Mathematics at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University, expecting to graduate in May 2026. My work spans reinforcement learning, optimization, cybersecurity, and autonomous systems, with a strong focus on building systems that are both theoretically grounded and deployable in real-world environments.

As a DoD SMART Scholar and intern at NSWCDD, I develop real-time autonomous object tracking systems that fuse YOLO-based detection, ORB keypoints, optical flow, re-identification, and Kalman filtering for high-energy weapon systems. At Embry-Riddle, I lead software R&D for an autonomous underwater vehicle in the Autonomous Maritime Robotics Association, integrating sonar, computer vision, and ML-based decision pipelines.
