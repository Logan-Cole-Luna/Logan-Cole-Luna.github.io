Bio / CV

I am a B.S. Computer Science graduate (Honors Program, magna cum laude) with a minor in Applied Mathematics from Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University (May 2026, cumulative GPA 3.85/4.00), and Embry-Riddle's Outstanding Undergraduate in Computer Science (2025 & 2026). In August 2026, I began an M.S. in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence specialization, thesis track) at the Georgia Institute of Technology. My research is in AI for Mathematical Reasoning (AI4Math): how semantically informative training signals can be quantified and incorporated into the training and generation of mathematical reasoning models.

At Georgia Tech's Reasoning and Learning Group under Dr. Vijay Ganesh, I contribute to AIProver, an agentic system that post-trains an open-weight LLM on graded verifier rewards to auto-formalize research mathematics. I built its RL pipeline and reward, its distillation pipeline and dataset, and its Semantic Alignment Model. I also coordinate Georgia Tech's Foundations of AI Seminar Series. As a DoW SMART Scholar and Scientist Intern (Active Secret clearance) at NSWCDD, I build real-time video detection and tracking systems. At Embry-Riddle, I researched RL-based satellite collision avoidance in the Agile Research Group and led autonomy software for the Autonomous Maritime Robotics Association's RoboSub team.
