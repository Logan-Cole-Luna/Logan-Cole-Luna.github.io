  • [C3] SensorWF: A FAIR Generalizable Workflow Framework for Scientific Time-Series Analysis
    Logan Luna, Joseph Rigo, Kellan Shew, Raul Alejandro Vargas-Acosta.
    IEEE eScience 2026 (ReWorDS Workshop), Naples, Italy. arXiv: 2609.21110
    Code: github.com/Logan-Cole-Luna/SensorWF
    One analytical core carried across spacecraft telemetry, ECG, and climate data with small domain adapters and no core changes; bit-identical outputs under fixed seeds; best-detector AUC-ROC of 0.772 on 18 injected fault types across 122 CubeSat channels (vs. 0.610 for a z-score baseline), 0.918 on ECG, and 0.835 on climate data.

Research Posters & Presentations

  • [P1] AERO-Sim2Real: Reinforcement Learning for Sim-to-Real Drone Deployment
    Logan Luna, Sirio Jansen-Sánchez, Vincent Reggi, Kolton Galie, Nicolas Andricos.
    Senior Design, Embry-Riddle, 2026.
    PPO policies trained in NVIDIA Isaac Lab and flown autonomously on Crazyflie 2.1 hardware; hover transferred at 1.3/2.1/0.7 cm real-vs-sim RMSE, while a figure-eight task isolated aggressive horizontal maneuvering as the dominant transfer gap.
  • [P2] Learning Causal Structures from Aviation Accident Narratives
    Madeline Gorman, Katherine Hoffsetz, Logan Luna, Stephanie Ramsey (alphabetical).
    Discovery Day, Embry-Riddle, 2025.
    Rule-based NLP, fine-tuned DistilBERT, and few-shot Mistral-7B compared for causal-triple extraction from 6,059 NTSB narratives; the LLM more than doubled narrative coverage (94.5% vs. 44.9%) without improving category alignment, separating coverage from correctness.

    Logan Luna (Autonomous Maritime Robotics Association).
    Student Research Symposium, Embry-Riddle, 2024.

  • [P4] Enhancing Aircraft Recognition and Localization through Object Detection and Feature Point Tracking with Kalman Filtering
    Logan Luna.
    National Conference on Undergraduate Research 2023; Student Research Symposium, Embry-Riddle, 2023. 1st Place, Student Research Symposium.
